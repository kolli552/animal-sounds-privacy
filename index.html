Privacy Policy

App: Kids Fun Animal Sounds

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Kids Fun Animal Sounds is a children's educational entertainment app featuring animal pictures, animal sounds and interactive feeding activities.

Information We Collect

The app does not require account registration. In its current version, it does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information.

How the App Works

Animal sounds, images and feeding interactions operate within the app. The app does not require children to submit personal information.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services.

Children's Privacy

We designed the app for children and aim to protect their privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information collected through the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact
